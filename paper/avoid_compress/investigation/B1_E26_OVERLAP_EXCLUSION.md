of F17 and F27. It retains all Boolean companions and exact shared

In each exact target atom the row generator injects outside-K inputs
and assigns guard inputs1,2 to every allowed target except those used

raw rows agree by a canonical SHA-256 hash, including empty atom groups.
Neither endpoint of E26,F17,F27 is individually protected.

Pure-letter cuts protect the literal family intersection in P7,
forbid outside-K pair merges through5, and retain every initial nine-set
whose powers are safe through11 and stay in the complete literal family
through5. These are necessary for any target with ac12, independent
of reaching paths. Exact empty row domains, not incoming capacities,
are used to justify any zero pair product.

Two complete checks use reverse pair-preimage closure and forward/
reverse state reachability, or scalar successor distance relaxation
and Boolean state transitive closure. Both reach genuine fixed points,
test synchronization and conditional short pairs, and separately run
cycle/identity, cycle/constant, identity/constant controls without the
conditional cuts. Positive candidates would additionally require safe
prefixes, Q-to-S reachability and exact local ac12. Uninvoked candidate
checks never establish a model. No timeout proves a negative.

The extent of exclusion is determined only by the actual complete
scan outputs and all-eight-type coverage. A binary two-row negative
cannot be transferred to an unrestricted alphabet.
