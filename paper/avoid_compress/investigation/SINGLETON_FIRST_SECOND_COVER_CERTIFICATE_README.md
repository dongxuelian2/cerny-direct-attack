# Singleton-first / second-cover certificate

This auxiliary package contains BINARY_SINGLETON_FIRST_SECOND_COVER.md,
its symbolic universal proof and an independent finite audit. The result
requires binary, singleton protected equality ac(S,{a})=n-1, s>=6,
|B|=1 and |C|>=3. If a fresh D has one point outside C, the C-merging
letter cannot be injective outside C. Consequently C cannot cover P3.
The proof uses the real affine equality flag and quotient reachability,
not a negative solver answer. Initial-set reachability is unnecessary.

With Python 3.10+ and the standard library, run:

```sh
python check_singleton_first_second_cover.py
```

The output must report pass, twelve finite type/geometry cases for
k=3,4,5, 185,664 quotient assignments and thirteen independently checked
singleton-first equality witnesses. The checker enumerates all outside
Boolean sets in its small common-fiber controls using integer augmented
rank and literal six-set intersections. It includes singular quotient
maps and every proper U size, checking the invariant-family or forbidden
D-count branches. These finite checks support the universal paper proof;
they do not enumerate all transition tables or prove all-k solely by data.

At (13,9,1), the proof excludes b1_out_c6 and b1_in_c5, and additionally
b1_out_c5_third1 with D={2}. The two original joint cases reduce the
remaining binary count to 26; unrestricted remains 43. After the previously
audited exclusions, 173 typed third orbits and 942 letter-profile pairs
remain in the original B1/B2 admission. Those are necessary cases, not
distinct automata. The earlier B3 and B2/C4 branch certificates are
separate prerequisites for these cumulative counts.

H covering P5 and |P1|<=4 at the thirteen-state parameter are premises;
their original proof notes are included but their larger earlier native
certificates are not rerun by this package. The original 235-orbit inventory
is retained intact. Both extrema remain [11,12], and the manuscript and
its accepted 109-file supplement are unchanged. No assertion is made for
s=5, larger alphabets, unrestricted multi-new-point D, or a new F value.
An actual reachable equality witness with two new third points is checked
as a boundary control rather than forced into the one-new-point quotient.
CHECKSUMS.txt authenticates the distributed files, and the audit regenerates
byte for byte from this standalone package.
