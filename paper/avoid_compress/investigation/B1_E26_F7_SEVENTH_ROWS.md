# Complete binary rows for the E26/F7 seventh survivors

Conditional hypotheses and complete seventh admission are specified in
B1_E26_F7_SEVENTH.md. Seven typed binary survivors require actual rows:
G8/H9,H19,H29; G18/H9; G28/H9,H18,H19.

The Python enumerator injects all inputs outside K={1,2} within each
exact output-membership atom, and assigns both guard inputs to every
output except those occupied by mandatory inputs. The separate C++
enumerator assigns sources 0 through12 recursively, retaining exactly
the same constraints. Both enumerate empty domains literally.

The mandatory core is the intersection of all literal nine-sets meeting
B,C,D,E,F,G,H once and containing3. Every actual image through four lies
in this family. Pure-letter powers must therefore stay in this family
through four and preserve rank9 and3 through eleven. Mandatory points
must be isolated in P7; outside-K pairs must not merge through five.
These are necessary conditions and do not require an initial Q-to-S
word for a negative result.

One complete coupled checker uses forward and reverse state reachability
and reverse pair-preimage closure to a fixed point. The other uses
Boolean state transitive closure and scalar forward pair-distance
relaxation to a fixed point, with a different rejection order. Bare
cycle/identity, cycle/constant and identity/constant controls disable
conditional cuts and check synchronization and strong connectivity.
Actual conditional domains enable the cuts. Raw-row hashes and every
retained row's initial-image mask must coincide independently.

Only completed scan results can justify a binary negative. Individual
row domains and two-letter pair scans cannot exclude a larger alphabet.
An uninvoked prefix/reaching check is not evidence of a model. A positive
candidate must pass exact local subset BFS, strong connectivity,
synchronization and actual Q-to-S reachability before being accepted.

The sources, data and audits here remain outside the accepted article
and its release. No new extremal value is asserted by this note alone.
