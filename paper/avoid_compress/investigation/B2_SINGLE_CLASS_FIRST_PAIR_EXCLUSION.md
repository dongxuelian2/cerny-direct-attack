# Five one-class B2 conditional exclusions

This certificate concerns only hypothetical extremizers with
(n,s,r,ac)=(13,9,1,12). It applies to strongly connected synchronizing
complete automata over any finite alphabet, and therefore also to binary
automata. It supplies no new numerical extremum or complete answer to
Open Problem 4.

The selected geometries are b2_mixed_c12_third4, b2_in_c1_third6,
b2_mixed_c1_third11, b2_mixed_c1_third9 and b2_mixed_c1_third10.
For each, every native immediate graph is independently reconstructed
by check_b1_b2_inverse_union; only one immediate graph class survives
the necessary supplier and strong-connectivity tests. Historical probe
data have their active-state hash matched to the immutable historical
baseline, and all other historical bindings are checked literally.

The proof premises, letter domains and equality-flag induction are
exactly those in B2_COLUMN_FIRST_PAIR_EXCLUSION.md and LOCAL_EQUALITY_FLAG.md.
The frozen starting authority here is AUXILIARY_AUDIT_20261011_04.json.
No graph-size cap or alphabet-size cap is added.

For the single retained graph we enumerate ALL ordered distinct feasible
initial nine-set images S,T. These span V1 for an actual extremizer.
Our relabelling preserves H,A,B,C,D, the mandatory intersection, and
every individual native preimage field. It simultaneously transports
the entire immediate graph, all exact native kernels, the P3 subgraph
and both images. The independent checker reconstructs the labelled
pairs, permutations and representative multiplicities. Extra symmetry
refinements change the number of representatives but do not remove a
single labelled pair.

For each representative, fresh literal maps are enumerated twice:
by output-atom bijections in the generator and by direct exact-block
assignments in the checker. Their full row and supplier-flag digests
must agree. Every next independent row is explored; required suppliers
and proper closed sets provide rejection witnesses. The checker uses
rational Fraction elimination, while the generator uses the previously
justified prime-modular elimination. Depth eleven survivors are
candidates, never exclusions. Safety at depth twelve is not required.

The final case audits and composition audit gate adoption. Counts from
QA, timeouts and incomplete prefixes cannot supply an exclusion. All
four prior binary profile-cut lists remain merged. A whole joint may
be removed only when every remaining third geometry belonging to it
has been excluded. The resulting structural counts do not determine
whether either extremum at (13,9,1) equals eleven or twelve.

The completed trees in this batch stop before depth four. Thus the
prefix-column rules are unused here. Their existing audit is a bound
premise of the common engine, not a claimed cause of these exclusions.
Excluding every initial S also excludes actual reachable images S;
the paper's reachability requirement has not been relaxed.
