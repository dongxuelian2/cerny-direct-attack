# B2-mixed C5 third3: complete first-pair actual-image staircase

Conditional scope is `(n,s,r,ac)=(13,9,1,12)`, for arbitrary finite
alphabets, strong connectivity and synchronization. This excludes only
the normalized geometry `b2_mixed_c5_third3`. It changes neither numerical
F bound nor the published partial-answer scope.

Use H={0,1,2}, A={3}, B={0,4}, C={1,2}, D={0,5}. The independently
audited inverse-union inventory supplies every possible immediate graph:
three of its seven classes have no required supplier, and four remain.
For each of those four take G=P1 union K_C union K_D, contained in P3.
The mandatory frame core is J={3}, isolated in P7. The full native fields
and exact kernels, all output-atom bijections and all output permutations
are retained; no alphabet-size bound is imposed.

Under equality12, the row spans of actual images of S through depth i

at A, hence lie in a12-dimensional linear subspace. Stabilization at any
earlier step would be permanent. Strong connectivity and synchronization
make the limiting row span all of R13: reset S and route its singleton
to every state. Thus growth cannot stop before12, or exceed one per step
through11. Rows through11 are characteristic vectors because safety
prevents every collision from S. These are the existing equality-flag
premises, not a new all-parameter bound.

Enumerate every ordered pair S,T of distinct nine-sets containing A,
meeting B,C,D once and independent in G. Every actual first-image span
has such a basis, and a line through two distinct Boolean vectors contains
no other Boolean vertex. Each letter therefore maps S to S or T, with
at least one supplier of S->T. Initial letters additionally map T to a
safe depth-two image, and supply pre(A)=B, pre(B)=C and pre(C)=D. These
four suppliers can coincide. The full labelled pair catalogue has12320

S,T; no triangle-plus-extra-edge assumption is used. Every pair is
transported to one of64 stored representatives. The checker verifies
every permutation, every field/kernel catalogue, the transported G and
the multiplicity of each representative. It need not certify that the
representatives are a minimal orbit transversal.

For each representative enumerate every literal letter in its initial
domain. The generator uses output colors and atom bijections. The
independent checker assigns exact kernel blocks directly to labelled

The earlier uniform inverse-domain criterion applies to arbitrary output
