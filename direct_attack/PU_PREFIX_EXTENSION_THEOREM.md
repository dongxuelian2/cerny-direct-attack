All words act on the right, in their written order. n>=2.

Let A be a strongly connected synchronizing PU automaton: each letter is
either a permutation or a unit (s->t), fixing every other state. Any finite
alphabet is allowed. Write G for the group generated by the permutation
letters. Its inverses are positive words because G is finite. Define Gamma
by all arcs (sg,tg), for every original unit (s->t) and g in G.

## Reachability prerequisite

G acts by automorphisms of Gamma. The union of its terminal strongly
connected components is G-invariant and preserved by all units. Strong
connectivity of A forces this union to be Q. Therefore every component of
Gamma is terminal, so there are no edges between different components.
Units preserve each component and permutations permute components. A
reset is impossible if there is more than one component. Hence Gamma is
strongly connected.

Conversely, if Gamma is strongly connected, every nonempty S is an actual
image Qw. Choose shortest Gamma paths toward S. For each vertex outside S,
choose the first arc of its path. Apply these conjugated units in descending
distance order; they move each outside vertex to its parent and leave S
fixed. The image becomes exactly S. A conjugated unit with arc (sg,tg) is
implemented by g^{-1} u g, a positive actual word. This also proves SC and
synchronization. Thus, under the stated SC hypothesis, synchronization is
equivalent to Gamma being strongly connected; Gamma-SC itself suffices
without a separate SC assumption. No analogous rejection criterion is
claimed for arbitrary non-SC PU automata.

## Exact maximum from at most n(n-1) ordered pairs

For s!=t let d(s,t) be the shortest length of a word v consisting only of
permutation letters such that some original unit arc (x,y) satisfies
(xv,yv)=(s,t). Set d=infinity outside Gamma. Put

    Gamma_l = {(s,t): d(s,t)+1 <= l}.

For every nonempty proper S, the actual-image extension cost is

    e(S) = 1 + min { d(s,t): s not in S, t in S }.                 (1)

Proof: a shortest inverse extension can end as soon as its size first
exceeds |S|. Before that, any neutral or shrinking inverse unit can be
deleted: removing it leaves a superset, and all later preimages are
monotone. If the modified process grows earlier, it is shorter still.
Since every nonempty subset is an actual image, the larger set at that
first growth is admissible. Thus all preceding inverse steps are
permutations, followed by one growing inverse unit. A unit changes size by
at most one. Written forward, the word is u v. Its preimage is larger
exactly when xv is outside S and yv is inside S. This proves (1).

It follows that

    max_{empty != S != Q} e(S)
      = min { l>=1 : Gamma_l is strongly connected }.             (2)

A strongly connected directed graph enters every nonempty proper subset.
If Gamma_l is not SC, it has a nonempty proper forward-closed set R;
S=Q\R has no entering arc and e(S)>l. These implications prove equality.
Multi-source BFS on ordered pairs computes d in polynomial time; no
enumeration of 2^n images is required. The extension cost charges u v,
NOT the longer conjugation v^{-1} u v used in the reachability proof.

## Failure of e(S)<=n in every odd dimension n>=5

Let n=2m+3, m>=1, L=n-2 odd. Use Q={0,...,n-1} and

    a=(0 1)(2 3 ... n-1),   b=(1 2),   u=(0 -> n-1).
    S={0,3,5,...,n-2}.

The permutation group is S_n. Indeed, a^L=(0 1), while a^2 fixes 0,1
and cycles {2,...,n-1} (gcd(2,L)=1). Conjugating b by its powers gives
all transpositions (1,q), q>=2; together with (0,1) they generate S_n.
Hence Gamma consists of every ordered distinct pair, and every nonempty
subset is reachable.

We prove e(S)=max_T e(T)=n+1 for all these n, independently of experiments.

For the lower bound, b preserves membership in S. Every a-transition flips
membership except n-1->2, whose endpoints are both outside S. Follow the
ordered pair (0,n-1) under a permutation word v. Before the first a, b
fixes the pair. The first a gives (1,2), both outside S. Membership then
remains equal until one coordinate takes an exceptional transition.

The source cannot take its first exceptional transition before n letters:
it must move 0->1 by a, enter the long cycle 1->2 by b, follow the L-1
successive a-edges from 2 to n-1, then take the exceptional a-edge. This
requires L+2=n steps. Detours through b at 1,2 cannot shorten the chain:
each vertex 3,...,n-1 has only its predecessor as an incoming transition
other than a self-loop. The target, after the first a, starts at 2; its
second exceptional transition requires at least L more steps, so occurs
no earlier than L+1=n-1. If it occurs before any source exception, both
coordinates are outside immediately before it; afterward the source is
inside and the target outside. This still is not a growing pair. Therefore
no word of length <=n-1 maps the unit arc to outside->inside S. The word
v=a^n does: it maps the pair to (1,3). Thus e(S)=n+1.

For the matching global upper bound, explicit arcs of Gamma_n give
reciprocal stars on the two parts S and B=Q\S:

* u a^j, even j=2,4,...,L-1: 0 to every other vertex of S.
* u a b a^j, odd j=1,3,...,L-2: every other vertex of S to 0.
* u a^j, odd j=1,3,...,L-2, and j=L: 1 to every other vertex of B.
* u a b a^j, even j=0,2,...,L-1: every other vertex of B to 1.

All have length <=n. The original u gives S->B. At length n+1, u a^n
adds B->S via 1->3. Thus Gamma_{n+1} is SC, proving (2)'s threshold n+1.
Gamma_n has exactly the two SC components S,B, with no B->S edge.
This is an infinite extension obstruction, not a counterexample to the
square reset bound. Observed rt=P=4n-10 for n5,7,9,11 is ONLY FINITE;
no all-n reset-threshold formula is asserted.

## State minimum five within ALL PU alphabets

**COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED finite cover with a proved reduction.**
No state minimum is asserted among unrestricted DFA alphabets.

Ignore identity/duplicate letters. An inclusion-minimal subset of the
permutation alphabet generating the same group is irredundant; deleting
other permutations cannot create a shorter extension. Irredundancy is
hereditary under subsets. At n4, all 8855 four-subsets of the 23
nonidentity permutations have a retained redundancy witness, independently
checked by tuple group closure. Hence every irredundant basis has <=3
letters. At n3, all three-subsets are redundant (the checked list has
5 singleton and 9 pair bases); larger irredundant sets are impossible.
At n2 there is just one nonidentity permutation.

For each basis and each cut cardinality k, relabel S to {0,...,k-1}.
Mark a unit safe if every permutation word of length <=n-1 maps its arc
away from the entering cut. Include ALL safe units. The full conjugate
graph of these units is certified non-SC by a forward-closed proper set.
There are 1,28,1233 cut designations for n2,3,4 respectively. Any purported
PU counterexample with e(S)>n has only safe unit seeds after restricting
to a minimal permutation basis. Its full conjugate graph remains SC
(same group and units), and adding safe units preserves SC. It would
therefore contradict one of the certified non-SC graphs. Pure-unit
alphabets have e=1 whenever Gamma is SC. This exhausts all finite PU
alphabets for n<=4. The displayed ternary n5 establishes the class minimum.

## Independent audit and literature boundary

data/pu_prefix contains 12 family cases n5..21,31,63,101, 186 previously
retained PU samples, four full ordinary-image arrays and the complete cut
cover. check_pu_prefix validates every pair-distance primal/Bellman
certificate, permutation closure, the SC upper graph and closed lower
cut. Its minimum-cover audit uses REVERSE pair BFS from the entering cut
and tuple group closure, unlike the generator's forward per-seed BFS and
indexed multiplication table. Ordinary reachable-image BFS independently
checks the small exact extension arrays. Six corruptions are rejected.
The all-n result rests on the paper proof, not these finite cases.

Rystsov conjugate graphs and PU complete reachability are known.
Szykula--Vorel (DLT2016) establish immediate-unit greediness; this is not
the stronger word-phase policy, which already fails at n4. Ferens--Szykula
(2026, DOI10.1145/3798283) give PU subset-extension bounds depending on
maximum group-orbit size, with general quadratic reset bounds. No new
general quadratic bound follows here. Priority of the exact prefix
threshold identity and the displayed obstruction remains OPEN.

Kisielewicz--Szykula, MFCS2015, arXiv1608.01268, Theorems1/2 give ordinary
quadratic extension obstructions. Section3.1 explicitly excludes their
difficult subsets from actual images and poses exactly the image-capacity
extension formulation used in this repository. That known obstruction
does not refute the present unrestricted actual-image e<=2n route. The
new PU family only refutes e<=n, and preserves complete reachability.
