synchronizing n-state DFA, c(S)<=2n(n-k+1)/(k-1), without rounding.
The protected-point cycle insertion family has k=n-3, c(S)=9, and unbounded
n with gcd(n-11,12)=1. Every eligible n>36 violates the proposal. This
forces a necessary unrounded coefficient C>=9/4. No state-minimality claim
is made. The rounded C=2 proposal remains OPEN: ceil(8n/(n-4))>=9.
Every rounded coefficient C<2 does fail eventually. See
CYCLE_INSERTION_THEOREM.md and data/cycle_insertion/.

**Q1 — REFUTED, arbitrary-alphabet SC state minimum n=4.** The proposed
additive quotient lift rt(A)<=rt(A/theta)+(b-1)^2, where b is the largest
congruence class, fails for a=[2,3,0,1], b=[1,0,0,0] and classes
{0,1}|{2,3}: rt(A)=3, rt(A/theta)=1, b=2. The valid all-class subset
count gives 1+M(theta)=3. At n<=3 the proposed lift holds for every
strongly connected synchronizing DFA by the singleton-class argument in
QUOTIENT_FIBER_LIFT.md; all alphabets up to three letters are also scanned.
This is not a counterexample to Cerny's square.

**Q2 — REFUTED, infinitely many states.** Replacing n-h+1 by n-h in
the short first-return alphabet theorem fails for the binary family in
SHORT_RETURN_QUOTIENT_LIFT.md, with h2 and every odd n>=5. Every shorter
return acts as a permutation; a constant return first appears at n-h+1.
This also attains the proved whole-class compression bound exactly.
The general-class cutoff statement already fails at its state minimum n3:
a=[2,2,0],b=[1,0,0],C={0,1}, with sole length-one return b and constant
return aa. Here the minimum class has size one. Requiring C to be a
minimum class still fails at its state minimum n4, with
a=[2,3,2,2],b=[1,0,0,1] and classes {0,1}|{2,3}: b and ab are
permutations, aab is constant. Direct SC/reset/return proofs are in
SHORT_RETURN_QUOTIENT_LIFT.md; no added 53-corpus certificate is claimed.

**T1 — REFUTED as a proposed proof route, all n>=4.** Optimizing the
terminal-wedge window b cannot make the independent rank sum quadratic
on C_n. Its exact pair spectrum is 1,...,binom(n,2), so terminal span

**A1 — REFUTED as a proposed averaging route, every n>=6.** A transitive
permutation group need not have an exact nonnegative uniform matrix
average J/n supported on original permutation words of length <=n-1.
For adjacent-transposition generators of S_n, total line displacement
is at most twice the word length, whereas the uniform average forces
expected displacement (n^2-1)/3. The exact minimum expected word cost
is (n^2-1)/6, attained by uniformly chosen cyclic shifts with literal
adjacent-transposition words. It exceeds n-1 at every n>=6. A singular
unit embeds this alphabet in a strongly connected synchronizing DFA;
the assertion still concerns permutation-only support. No claim about
singular-word averages, one-state averages, image extension, the square,
exact maximum-support optimum or minimum refutation size follows.
See PERMUTATION_AVERAGING_WORD_COST.md and its independent audit.
