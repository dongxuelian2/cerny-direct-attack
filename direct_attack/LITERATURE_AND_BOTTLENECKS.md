full publisher text is subscription-only. [Rystsov 2025, section 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.19166)
defines regular as transitive and semiregular and treats a single simple
idempotent with such a group. This does not directly cover unequal disjoint
cycles with arbitrary unit sets. [Ferens--Szykula 2026](https://doi.org/10.1145/3798283)
confirms known PU complete reachability and quadratic class bounds.
Circular/trivial-group conclusions are reconstructed known theory; priority
of the mixed-cycle n-c+1 bound and all-tie equality remains OPEN.

The positive polynomial product_i(1+x+...+x^(l_i-1)) averages every a-cycle
exactly and has degree n-c, even for permutation order 360360. No delayed
unit growth forces cycle means to decrease along a strongly connected unit
quotient, hence all equal; positivity gives zero gradients and a recurrence
propagates them to every conjugate. This closes the square for ONE

nor a simultaneous averaging operator for noncommuting generators.

no minimum across arbitrary alphabets is asserted.

## Prefix graphs and ordinary versus actual extension

## Congruence lifting and the simple-automaton bottleneck

[Rodaro--Venturi (2026), Proposition 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.17992)
proves the square lifts from a quotient of a strongly connected automaton
whenever that congruence has a class of size one or two. Their general
hereditariness problem remains open. Their 7/48 cubic coefficient is
conditional on a quotient already satisfying the square, not an improved
general cubic bound. QUOTIENT_FIBER_LIFT.md adds an elementary conditional
count of all non-singleton subsets inside congruence classes. It excludes
profiles with every class size 3..6. A rank-truncated variant independently
reconstructs the published 1/2-class qualitative lift. Together these say
that a hypothetical state-minimal counterexample may have a nontrivial
congruence only if one class has size at least seven. Simple automata have
no such congruence, so this does not resolve the hard general case. The
precise counting lemma's publication priority has not been established.
For at least three quotient states, the same count raises that necessary
class size to eight.
The truncated bound additionally handles the two-class (3,7) profile,
which the full-subset count alone leaves open. The pair-truncated variant
also handles (4,7) and shows that any hypothetical minimal-counterexample
congruence has EVERY class of size at least four. No conclusion about all
seven-state-class profiles follows. Priority of these precise bounds is
unsettled; they are not an unrestricted synchronization bound.
